Both used the same saved refined prompt, seed 42050, CFG 1 and cache setting. The blue eyes, skin detail, dark hair and soft window light held up well, with small face/framing differences. Equal seeds do not guarantee identical composition across checkpoints; this single comparison is not a general speed or quality guarantee.
