# ALi Imagine 0.9.40-beta.3 — preview

Explore now sends one starting image to LTX Orbit instead of repeating the same image at both ends. Fit framing uses the option consumed by the native engine. Orbit controls and review guidance explicitly mark the feature experimental. Older jobs retain their original recipes when retried.

The observatory diagnostic completed in 5 min 38 sec and did not show the earlier mosaic breakup, but it still showed partial camera movement and changing lighting. This release does not establish reliable full 360-degree coverage or frozen geometry. Review side and back views before building a 3D scene.

Includes the earlier CEL Character Steps slider (2–60, default 30), optional LTX exposure stabilization and upscale join exposure matching (both off by default), Qwen Turbo, Full and Unlocked, Qwen 2×, and existing image/video workflows. The eight-step CEL comparison was cancelled; its quality and speed have not been established.

Known issue: the completion-refresh race that can leave Gallery stale is not repaired in this preview. Completed output files and registration may exist even while the view is stale.

This is a manually installed preview. The stable update feed remains at 0.9.39. Model terms, setup and H3 authorization requirements are unchanged.
