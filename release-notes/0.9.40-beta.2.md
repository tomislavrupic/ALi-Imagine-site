# ALi Imagine 0.9.40-beta.2 — preview

CEL Character now has a Steps slider from 2–60. The author recipe remains 30 steps by default; lower counts such as 8 are experiments for speed and may reduce detail, identity stability or motion consistency. Requests, replay, resolved recipes and output metadata retain the chosen count. Only the 30-step, strength-1, guidance-1 recipe may certify the model. Historical requests retain their settings.

Includes optional experimental LTX exposure stabilization and source-relative exposure matching at native pixel 2× joins. Both default off. They address small brightness variation and do not guarantee flicker-free output.

Includes Qwen Turbo Q8, Full and Unlocked, model-specific Qwen 2×, Refine and raw prompting, and the earlier FLUX, Explore, paired-Mac and video features. Existing projects and model files are preserved. Model terms and setup requirements remain unchanged; H3 execution remains authorization-gated.

Known issue: a completion-refresh race may leave the Gallery view stale after a render finishes. This preview does not include that repair. Completed media can remain saved and registered even while the view is stale.

Preview status: custom CEL step counts are experimental; the eight-step visual/speed comparison is still in progress. The stable updater feed remains at 0.9.39. Install this preview manually from its DMG.
